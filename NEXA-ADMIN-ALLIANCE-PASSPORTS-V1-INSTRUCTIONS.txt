VERIFY

- Administration shows Alliance Passports and no separate Roles tab.
- Alliance cards appear as planets.
- Opening a planet shows Game Power, NEXA Registered Power, server rank and members.
- R5, R4 and R3–R1 appear in separate groups.
- Owner/Admin can edit all alliance ranks.
- R5 can assign R4–R1 in their alliance.
- R4 can assign R3–R1 in their alliance.
- Nobody can change their own rank.
- Operational roles save independently.
- Only Owner can search a Game ID and assign/remove Admin under Permissions.
- Owner is protected.
- Owner/Admin/R5 can schedule an alliance deletion.
- The alliance deactivates immediately and shows a 24-hour countdown.
- Cancel Deletion restores it.
- After the deadline, the alliance is removed when NEXA next processes Alliance Passports.
- Its players remain intact under the Unassigned planet.
