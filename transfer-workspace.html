Transfer Application

Applicants • Simple Flow

New Applicants stay in the main view until Transfer Staff assigns an alliance and chooses a route.

  • Green card — within the Ordinary Power Cap.
  • Gold card — above the Ordinary Power Cap / possible Special route.
  • GROUP badge — the applicant belongs to a transfer group. Group is a view, not a route.
  • Game ID + Discord — tap either line to copy it.
  • Assigned Alliance — required before choosing Ordinary or Special.
  • Route — tap Ordinary or Special. That choice moves the applicant to that folder automatically.
  • Invite — Pending means not sent yet; Sent marks the invitation as sent.
  • Double tap the card — opens the submitted questions and answers. The View button does the same thing.

Clear All Eligible Applicants

Clear All does not permanently delete applicants. Ordinary and Special applicants from the finished cycle remain inside Transfer History. Every active New Application and Not Selected applicant moves to Applicant Archive, including restored applicants. Next Transfer Cycle is not affected.
OWNER / ACCESS MANAGEMENT

End Transfer Cycle

This closes the active Transfer Cycle and moves its cycle record to History. It does not move, archive, or delete any applicants. Applicant placements remain unchanged. Afterward, a new Transfer Cycle can be opened.
