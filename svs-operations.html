NEXA Battle Guide

Workspace & Mobile View

Strategy Board is your editable Team Builder. On mobile, Team cards move sideways so they stay readable. Swipe left/right to inspect every Team. Desktop can show much more at once. Use Team Layout for the complete final sheet.

NEXA Assist & Manual Mode

Choose Alliance, number of Teams, battle focus and data source. NEXA Assist creates a draft. You can still move, add or delete Teams, Rally Leaders and Joiners manually afterward.

Rally Leaders, Joiners & Heroes

Rally Leaders stay at the top with schedule and PETS. Joiners appear below with Attack A/B and Defense A/B. Join First marks the first four. Rally Lead hero formations are managed once per Alliance below the Teams.

Pet Schedule & Formations

Pet Schedule rows can come from Teams, but NEXA never decides Garrison, Handoff or Counter for you. Violet blocks mean PETS active. Attack and Defense formation presets can be added separately and stacked downward.

Team Layout, Preview & Download

Team Layout shows the complete final sheet. On mobile use Preview Layout and, for the clearest view, rotate your phone sideways. Preview fits the sheet to your screen; Download exports the full NEXA-styled layout.
