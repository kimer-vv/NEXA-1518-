Forms Center

Forms Center is NEXA’s central hub for event sign-ups, applications and surveys. It keeps forms organized by activity and lets you open or share them even when the related event is not currently live.

When a form is connected to NEXA, signed-in players can use their WOS Account and profile information to make submissions faster. Leadership can review those responses and use them in the corresponding NEXA workflow.
