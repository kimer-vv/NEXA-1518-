NEXA Battle Guide

Workspace & Mobile View

Strategy Board is your editable Team Builder. On mobile, Team cards move sideways so they stay readable. Swipe left/right to inspect every Team. Desktop can show much more at once. Use Team Layout for the complete final sheet.

NEXA Assist & Manual Mode

Live Auto Generate uses Battle Roles + Responses + Profile/Inventory automatically. Only Test Sandbox asks for Rally Leader and Joiner counts. NEXA decides how many sustainable Teams the roster can actually fill. Manual Build leaves every assignment to you and NEXA reviews it when you press Done.

Rally Leaders, Joiners & Heroes

Rally Leaders come from one finite global pool. They may rotate between Alliances only when time blocks do not overlap. PETS use one continuous 2-hour window on whole-hour boundaries. Joiners remain fixed unless you move them; duplicate assignments trigger a warning. Every Rally Leader and Joiner can be removed individually.

Pet Schedule & Formations

Team 1 is the Primary Pet Team. Team 2 becomes Secondary PET / Support only when real Rally Leader depth allows it; Teams 3+ are normally strong mixed teams without PETS. NEXA also varies the first four Joiner heroes when complementary buffs are more useful than duplicates.

Team Layout, Preview & Download

Team Layout shows the complete final sheet. On mobile use Preview Layout and, for the clearest view, rotate your phone sideways. Preview fits the sheet to your screen; Download exports the full NEXA-styled layout.
