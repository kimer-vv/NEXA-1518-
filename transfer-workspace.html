Transfer Application

How to use Applicants

Folders organize applicants: Inbox, Ordinary, Special, Not Selected and Next Transfer Cycle. Tap any card to open the full read-only application. On desktop/tablet you can drag a card into another folder. On mobile, use Move to inside the applicant detail. Alliance Allocation assigns a recruiting alliance. Invite Sent tracks whether the invitation has been sent. Blue cards are within the Ordinary Power Cap; gold cards are above it. Gold does not automatically mean Special Invite—it means the applicant needs the special route or a power reduction. Next Transfer Cycle is used by automatic deadline routing. Only ★ Access Management can permanently delete an application.
