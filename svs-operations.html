NEXA Battle Guide

Workspace & Mobile View

Team Board is your editable Team Builder. On mobile, Team cards move sideways so they stay readable. Swipe left/right to inspect every Team. Desktop can show much more at once. Use Team Layout for the complete final sheet.

Manual Team Board

Team Board follows the Alliances and Teams created in Pet Schedule. Add Players manually from the NEXA-ranked Joiner list, configure roles, troop ratios and hero pools, then review the final layout.

Rally Leaders, Joiners & Heroes

Rally Leaders come from one finite global pool. They may rotate between Alliances only when time blocks do not overlap. PETS use one continuous 2-hour window on whole-hour boundaries. Joiners remain fixed unless you move them; duplicate assignments trigger a warning. Every Rally Leader and Joiner can be removed individually.

Pet Schedule & Formations

PETS are organized by Main Garrison Alliance and Counter Alliances. Each Alliance may contain as many Teams as needed. Every Team keeps one fixed NEXA color so the schedule is easy to scan. NEXA preserves the evidence-backed Top 4 Joiner stack and uses vetted emergency backups when available; Top 4 value is based on how the four joiner effects complement and stack together, not on isolated skill ranking.

Team Layout, Preview & Download

Team Layout is the final-sheet preview. Rally Lead Attack/Defense formations keep hero portraits and ratios at the top. Joiner rows intentionally show names only so the text can be larger and cleaner. Preview and Download use the same layout.
