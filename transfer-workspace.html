How to use Applicants

Use the four folders to organize the applications selected for Transfer. New/unclassified applications appear in the default New Applications view.

  • Ordinary — applicants assigned to the Ordinary transfer route.
  • Special — applicants actually assigned to a Special Invite.
  • Not Selected — applicants not selected for this Transfer cycle.
  • Next Transfer Cycle — applications saved for the next cycle, including automatic post-deadline routing.
  • Blue card — the applicant is within the Ordinary Power Cap.
  • Gold card — the applicant is above the Ordinary Power Cap. Gold does not automatically mean Special Invite; staff may use a power reduction or another route.
  • Move to — changes the applicant folder directly from the card.
  • Assign Alliance — assigns one active Recruiting Alliance directly from the card.
  • Invite status — marks whether the invite has been sent.
  • Tap the card — opens the full application in read-only mode.
  • Desktop / tablet — drag and drop is kept as a convenience where the browser supports it. Mobile never depends on drag and drop.
